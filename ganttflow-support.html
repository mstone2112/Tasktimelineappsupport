Task Timeline Gantt User Guide

Thank you for using Task Timeline Gantt! This app gives you a full project planning toolkit — Gantt timelines, Kanban boards, milestones, dependencies, and multi-format export — in a single universal app that works natively on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

The Four Tabs

Timeline

The Timeline tab is the heart of the app. It shows all of your tasks laid out on a Gantt chart so you can see your entire plan at a glance. On this tab you can:

  • Add a new task using the + button
  • Group tasks by:
    • Project
    • Owner
    • Category
    • Status
    • Priority
  • Use the arrow next to the Group By button to reverse the sort direction
  • Sort tasks within each group by:
    • Date
    • Status
    • Priority
  • Adjust the zoom level of the timeline:
    • Use the preset buttons: Fit, Quarter, or Detailed
    • Pinch on an empty area of the chart to zoom in or out freely
  • Adjust task timing directly on the chart:
    • Long press a task bar to reveal the move handle and date resize handles
    • Drag the bar itself to shift the entire task to a new date range
    • Drag either end handle to extend or shorten the task's duration

Milestones

A task with the same start and end date is treated as a milestone. Instead of a bar, it renders as a diamond (◆) on the timeline. Milestones are useful for marking fixed delivery dates, reviews, or key checkpoints. You can drag a milestone left or right to move it to a different date — there are no resize handles since a milestone has no duration.

Dependencies

Tasks can be linked to show that one must finish before another can begin. Dependency arrows are drawn automatically between connected tasks on the timeline, including to and from milestone diamonds. To add or remove a dependency, open the task detail panel and use the Links section.

Kanban

The Kanban tab gives you a card-based view of the same tasks, organized by status. Use it when you want to focus on what's in progress rather than when things are scheduled. Drag a card from one column to another to update its status — the change is reflected immediately on the Timeline as well.

Tasks

The Tasks tab lists all your tasks in a scrollable list for quick inline editing. On this tab you can:

  • Tap any field to edit it directly — dates, names, status, priority, and more
  • Create new projects, categories, or owners by selecting that field and typing a new name
  • Delete tasks or remove individual attributes inline

Settings

  • Choose an accent color from an extensive palette to customize the look of the timeline
  • Set your preferred default grouping, zoom level, and sort order so the Timeline opens exactly the way you like it

Export

Task Timeline Gantt can export your plan in three formats from the Export button in the Timeline tab:

Format Best for
PDF Printing or attaching to documents and emails
PNG Dropping your chart into a presentation or report
Excel (.xlsx) Sharing task data with colleagues using Microsoft Excel or Apple Numbers

On iPhone and iPad, exported files are delivered via the native share sheet so you can save to Files, AirDrop, email, or any other app. On Mac, a native Save dialog lets you choose exactly where to save the file.

All exports are generated entirely on your device. No data is sent to any server.

iCloud Sync

Your projects sync automatically across all your Apple devices via iCloud. Make a change on your iPhone and it will appear on your iPad and Mac within moments. Sync uses your own private iCloud account — the developer never has access to your data.

Universal Purchase

Task Timeline Gantt is a single purchase that covers iPhone, iPad, and Mac. The Mac version is a full desktop experience with a native fixed side panel, keyboard-friendly interactions, and native save dialogs — not a scaled-up phone app.

Design Philosophy

Task Timeline Gantt is built for people who need a real project planning tool — not a simple to-do list, but also not an enterprise platform with a learning curve. The goal is to make Gantt charts, dependencies, and milestones feel approachable and fast to use, whether you're managing a product launch, a home renovation, or a team project. Everything you see on the timeline reflects the same underlying data you edit in the task list and move around on the Kanban board.

Updates

Task Timeline Gantt continues to grow. Here's what's new:

Quick Add

The + button now opens Quick Add — a plain, unformatted list view built for fast structural edits to your plan. From this screen you can:

  • Drag a task vertically to reorder it
  • Use the arrow icons to indent or outdent a task, creating summary tasks
  • Select a task's link icon and drag a connector to another task's link icon to set up a dependency between them
  • Press Enter after saving a task to automatically open a new row beneath it for rapid entry

"Tasks" Grouping Renamed to "None"

The Group By option formerly labeled "Tasks" is now called None, for clarity — it isn't really a grouping. Behavior is unchanged: tasks still display in start date order.

Indenting and Linking Directly in Timeline

The structural controls from Quick Add are now available in the Timeline view as well. Selecting a task row reveals indent and outdent arrows. Long-pressing a task bar until a dot appears lets you drag out a connector line to link it to another task, the same gesture used in Quick Add.

Summary Tasks Are Unlinkable by Design

Because summary tasks are derived from their subtasks, they cannot be directly linked to other tasks, and they do not appear in the Kanban or Calendar views. This is intentional — task management is meant to happen at the individual task level, not the summary level.

Summary Task Appearance

In the Manage view, you can choose how summary tasks are displayed: the standard bracket appearance, or a new style that shows the aggregate percent complete across all of a summary task's subtasks.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

Task Timeline Gantt respects your privacy. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data with the developer or any third party. All task and project information you enter is stored locally on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, synced through your own private iCloud account.

Information We Do Not Collect

Task Timeline Gantt does not collect or process any of the following:

  • Personal information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or address)
  • The contents of your tasks, projects, or plans
  • Device information
  • Usage data or analytics
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, or files
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Cookies or tracking technologies

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or external data services of any kind.

Data Storage and Sync

All task and project data you create in the app is stored locally on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, that data is also synced through your own private CloudKit database tied to your iCloud account. This data is never transmitted to the developer or to any server operated by the developer. Apple's standard iCloud account security governs access to it. If you delete the app, all locally stored data is removed from your device.

Third-Party Services

Task Timeline Gantt does not integrate with or send data to any third-party services. iCloud sync is provided directly by Apple as part of your iCloud account.

Children's Privacy

Because the developer does not collect any personal data, Task Timeline Gantt is safe for users of all ages. No personal information is knowingly collected from children.

Changes to This Policy

If the app's functionality changes in the future in a way that affects data handling, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly. Any changes will be posted on this page.
